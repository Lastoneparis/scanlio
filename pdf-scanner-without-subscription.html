Is there a PDF scanner app without a subscription?

Scanlio is a free-to-download document scanner and PDF editor for iPhone, iPad and Mac (iOS 17, iPadOS 17 or macOS 14 and later) that processes every scan on-device, with no account required.

Short answer: yes, but you have to read the purchase screen carefully. Most scanner apps on the App Store are free to install and then sell a subscription. A few also offer a one-time purchase, and Apple's own Notes and Files apps scan for nothing at all. This page lays out, honestly, how Scanlio is priced, what you get without paying, and how the other well-known scanner apps handle the same question, using only what their App Store listings say.

Why people are tired of scanner subscriptions

A scanner is a tool you use in bursts: a week of paperwork when you move house, a pile of receipts at tax time, one lease a year. Paying every month for something you use twice a quarter feels wrong, and a weekly plan that quietly renews is worse. The frustration is not with paying for good software; it is with paying forever for software that does not change much between uses. That is why "PDF scanner without subscription" is such a common search.

How Scanlio is priced

Scanlio is free to download. There are three layers, and it helps to be precise about each.

  • Free. Every tool in the app shares the same free allowance, and saving your scans is never blocked. Scanning with edge detection, importing PDFs and images, organizing the library and sharing all work without paying. On iPhone and iPad the free version shows non-personalized ads through Google AdMob; Scanlio does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across apps. The Mac version shows no ads.
  • Scanlio Pro as a subscription. Pro unlocks unlimited OCR, the full PDF editor, e-signatures, format conversions and unlimited exports, and removes ads. It is offered weekly, monthly and yearly, with a free trial on the yearly plan whose length is shown on the purchase screen. Prices are listed on the App Store at the time of writing and appear in your local currency at checkout.
  • Scanlio Pro as a one-time purchase. The App Store listing also includes a Lifetime in-app purchase, listed at $119.99 in the US store at the time of writing. If you never want a renewing plan, the Lifetime option is the one to look for on the purchase screen. It is a single payment, not a subscription, so nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.

The listing also shows a "Scanlio Cloud" monthly and yearly purchase. It is not described in the App Store text, so we do not make claims about it here; the core app works entirely offline and needs no cloud.

What "free" really covers in Scanlio

Some apps let you scan for free and then refuse to save or export until you pay, which makes the free tier a demo. Scanlio's approach, as described in its 1.0.17 release notes, is that every tool shares one free allowance and saving is never blocked. You can build a library of scans, keep them on your device, and share them. The Pro features (unlimited OCR, the full editor, signatures, conversions and unlimited exports) are where the allowance applies, and Pro removes it.

How to check for a trial before you pay

The Scanlio purchase screen leads with the plans and shows whether a free trial is attached to the one you pick. Apple also prints the trial length and the renewal price on the confirmation sheet before you approve with Face ID. If a trial is running, Scanlio reminds you before it ends, a change introduced in version 1.0.17. Any unused trial period is forfeited if you buy a plan outright, as the App Store description notes.

How to cancel

  1. Open the App Store app and tap your account picture.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Select Scanlio and tap Cancel.

You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. The Lifetime purchase has no renewal, so there is nothing to cancel; if you ever reinstall, use Settings then Restore Purchases in Scanlio to bring it back.

What happens to your files if you stop paying

Nothing happens to them. Scanlio never uploaded your documents to a server, so there is no account to lapse and no cloud folder to lose access to. Your PDFs stay in the app on your device and in your own iCloud or device backups. You simply return to the free allowance for Pro features, and saving is never blocked. This is a meaningful difference from apps that store your library in their cloud: with those, the question of what you can still reach after cancelling is worth asking before you upload.

Choosing between Lifetime, yearly and free

  • You scan a few pages a month. Stay on free. Scanning, saving, organizing and sharing cost nothing.
  • You have a season of paperwork. A monthly or yearly Pro plan covers unlimited OCR, signatures and conversions while you need them; cancel afterwards and keep your files.
  • You want to buy once and forget it. The Lifetime purchase is the no-renewal path. At the US price listed at the time of writing it is roughly the cost of five years of the yearly plan, so it pays off for people who know they will keep using a scanner.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Scanlio without paying anything?

Yes. Scanlio is free to download, every tool shares the same free allowance, and saving your scans is never blocked. The free iPhone and iPad version shows non-personalized ads; the Mac version has none.

Does Scanlio have a one-time purchase instead of a subscription?

Yes. Alongside the weekly, monthly and yearly Scanlio Pro subscriptions, the App Store listing includes a one-time Lifetime purchase, listed at $119.99 in the US at the time of writing. Look for the Lifetime option on the purchase screen.

What happens to my documents if I stop paying for Pro?

Your documents stay on your device; Scanlio never stored them on a server in the first place. You return to the free allowance, and saving is never blocked.

How do I cancel a Scanlio subscription?

Open the App Store, tap your account, choose Subscriptions, select Scanlio and tap Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.

Is there a completely free scanner on iPhone?

Yes. Apple's Notes and Files apps include a built-in document scanner at no cost. It lacks an ID mode, PDF to Word conversion, white-out and merge or split tools, which is where a dedicated app such as Scanlio comes in.
