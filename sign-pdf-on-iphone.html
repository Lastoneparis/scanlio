How to sign a PDF on iPhone, iPad and Mac

Scanlio scans paper, ID cards and passports into PDFs with automatic edge detection, recognizes text with multilingual OCR, lets you sign, annotate, white-out, merge and split PDFs, and converts PDF to Word, images or plain text; Scanlio Pro (a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription, or the one-time Lifetime purchase listed on the App Store) unlocks unlimited OCR, editing, signing, conversion and exports and removes ads.

Someone emails you a lease, a quote or a school form and asks for it back signed today. You do not need a printer. Every iPhone, iPad and Mac can sign a PDF, and the only real question is which tool gives you the cleanest result with the least friction. This guide walks through the three common options on Apple devices, then shows the full signing workflow in Scanlio, including the parts that other tools tend to leave out: dating the document, filling in names, and covering a wrong entry before you sign.

Three ways to sign a PDF on an iPhone

  • Apple Markup. Open a PDF in Files or from a Mail attachment, tap the pen icon, choose the signature tool and draw. It is free and built in. Markup is fine for a one-off signature, but it does not scan paper documents for you, has no white-out, and its text tool is basic. Apple documents the feature in its iPhone User Guide.
  • Adobe Scan plus Acrobat Reader. Adobe Scan captures the page; according to its App Store listing you then open the scan in the separate Acrobat Reader app to fill forms and sign. Two apps, and scans are saved automatically to Adobe Document Cloud.
  • Scanlio. One app scans the paper, recognizes the text, lets you sign with a stored signature, add text boxes and white-out, and exports the finished PDF. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is required.

Signing a PDF in Scanlio, step by step

  1. Get the document into Scanlio. If it is on paper, tap the camera button and line it up; automatic edge detection crops the page and you can add more pages for a multi-page contract. If it arrived as a file, use the "+" menu to import the PDF or images from Files or Photos.
  2. Open Markup. Open the document and choose Markup. This is where signatures, text boxes, highlights, freehand drawing and white-out all live.
  3. Draw your signature once. Pick the signature tool and sign with your finger or, on iPad, with Apple Pencil. Scanlio keeps this as a reusable signature, so the next document takes a tap rather than another attempt at your handwriting.
  4. Place and size it. Drag the signature onto the signature line, then resize it to fit the box. Landscape forms often have small signature fields; shrink the signature rather than letting it overlap the text.
  5. Add the date and any names. Add a text box for the date, your printed name, or an initial in the margin. Text boxes in Scanlio can be moved, resized and recolored, so a dark blue "6 September 2026" next to a blue signature looks like it was written with the same pen.
  6. Cover mistakes with white-out. If a field was pre-filled incorrectly, use white-out to cover the old text and type the correction in a text box over it. This is the step Markup alone cannot do.
  7. Export and send. Save, then share as PDF via Mail, AirDrop, WhatsApp or any other app. The signed file is a normal PDF that opens anywhere.

What makes a reusable signature useful

The first time you sign in an app, you spend a minute getting the strokes right. The value comes afterwards: a stored signature means the tenth form of the month is signed in seconds, and every copy of your signature looks identical. Because Scanlio keeps that signature on the device, it is available offline and never passes through a server.

Is an electronic signature valid?

In many jurisdictions, simple electronic signatures, a drawn signature placed on a document, are accepted for everyday paperwork: quotes, rental agreements, permission slips, consent forms, freelance contracts. Some documents, such as certain deeds, wills or regulated financial instruments, may require a specific type of signature or a witnessed process. If a document matters legally, check the requirements that apply to your country and to the document type. This page is general information, not legal advice.

Common signing problems and fixes

  • The signature looks jagged. Sign larger than you need, then shrink it. Downscaling smooths the strokes; upscaling exaggerates them.
  • The form is a photo, not a PDF. Import the image into Scanlio; it becomes a PDF page you can sign and export like any scan.
  • The recipient wants the pages in a different order. Open Pages to reorder, rotate or delete pages, or merge several documents before signing.
  • You need the text of the contract as well. Use Convert to export to Word or plain text after signing; OCR keeps the document searchable.
