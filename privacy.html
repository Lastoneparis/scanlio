Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Scanlio ("we", "us") is built privacy-first. This policy explains what we do — and mostly don't — with your data.

Your documents stay on your device

All scanning, text recognition (OCR), editing, conversion and storage happen locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, view or store the documents, images or text you create in Scanlio. They never reach our servers.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, email or sign-in.
  • We do not collect or sell your documents or personal files.

Advertising (free version)

The free version on iPhone and iPad shows ads via Google AdMob. The macOS and Apple Vision Pro versions contain no ads at all. Scanlio Pro removes all ads.

Consent (EEA and UK). Before any ad is requested, Scanlio shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) wherever consent is required — in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. No ad is loaded until you have answered it. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Manage Privacy Choices.

App Tracking Transparency and the advertising identifier (IDFA). After the consent step, Scanlio presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you personalized ads, which can involve tracking across other apps and websites. If you decline — or simply dismiss the prompt — the identifier is not accessed and AdMob serves non-personalized ads only. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

To deliver ads, AdMob may also process a device signal, approximate (coarse) location and usage/diagnostic data, in accordance with Google's policies.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple processes transactions per its own privacy policy.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because Scanlio does not collect, store or share your documents or personal data on our servers, we hold no personal data about you to access, correct, export or delete. Your files live only on your device and in your own iCloud/backups, which you control. For data processed by Google (ads) or Apple (purchases), please exercise your rights directly with those providers under their policies. We do not sell personal information. Cross-app tracking for advertising happens only if you explicitly allow it at the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and only through AdMob — you can withdraw that permission at any time in iOS Settings, and change your advertising consent in Settings › Manage Privacy Choices.

Data retention

We do not retain your documents or personal data — there is nothing stored on our side. Documents remain on your device until you delete them.

Permissions

  • Camera — to scan documents (only when you scan).
  • Photos — only the images you choose to import or export.
  • Notifications — optional reminders you can turn off anytime.

Children

Scanlio is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here.
